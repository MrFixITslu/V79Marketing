# V79Marketing: Evidence-led creative & growth playbook (27 October 2026)

**Status:** proposed product guidance; not a claim that any provider integration, tracking or analytics is already operational.

## Promise

Help a small business turn **one real offer + one specific audience + one desired customer action**
into credible, channel-appropriate marketing. Publishing, enquiry capture and subscription conversion
are separate outcomes and must never be confused.

Target onboarding journey: V79 Hub sign-in → Marketing entitlement → V79Marketing campaign
planning → owner review → authorised distribution → verified measurement → next experiment.
For Tiquet campaigns specifically, show *actual* Hub and Tiquet screens and explain that
Tiquet is launched from Hub; do not promise unsupported integrations or features.

## Standard campaign brief

- **Business result:** choose one: enquiries, demo requests, qualified website visits, purchase,
  returning customers, or awareness. Measure these separately.
- **Audience and pain:** identify the customer and the specific daily problem.
- **Claim and proof:** state only verified product capabilities, real prices and eligible offer terms.
  Do not invent testimonials, screenshots, discounts, integrations, endorsements or data.
- **Next action:** one clear CTA, aligned to the landing destination and customer journey.
- **Distribution:** choose an appropriate channel and format rather than posting identical copy everywhere.
- **Measurement:** campaign ID, platform, attributable URL when appropriate, and the real event
  used to count success. Differentiate inferred attribution from provider-confirmed events.

## Creative review: six questions

1. Can the audience identify the problem/benefit in the opening?
2. Is this specific to the audience rather than generic AI output?
3. Can each factual claim and visual be verified against a current product build?
4. Is the next action unambiguous and possible on the destination page?
5. Does the creative suit the chosen channel, safe area, device and accessibility needs?
6. Can the team later tell whether the campaign led to a meaningful customer action?

If any answer is no or unknown, **revise the draft** instead of manufacturing evidence or results.

## 2026 operating principles

- Human-reviewed, authentic demonstrations and real customer language over slick but unverified imagery.
- Short-form, vertical video as a hypothesis to test, *not* a guarantee for every audience.
  For LinkedIn B2B, also test an educational post or proof-led static carousel.
- Benefits before features; specific hooks and calls to action. Ads and landing pages must match.
- Two differentiated creative variants per campaign to learn which message resonates.
  Do not present A/B testing as statistically conclusive when sample sizes are small.
- Prefer depth on a few reliable channels over incomplete distribution on many platforms.
- Preserve evidence of provider success, failures, channel permissions, version and actual timestamps.
- No default auto-publication, paid ad spend, customer messaging, fabricated results, or
  cross-tenant account access.

## Pilot: Vision79 Digital → Hub → Tiquet

**Audience:** Saint Lucia service businesses managing customer requests manually.

**Hypothesis:** a real 25–40 second screen-recorded walkthrough, starting with the customer's
follow-up problem and showing Hub → Tiquet access, may generate more qualified demo enquiries
than a feature-only static image.

**Variant A — real walkthrough:** problem hook → Hub login and app selection → genuine Tiquet
workflow → a single approved CTA to request access/demo.

**Variant B — problem/solution carousel:** three customer pain points → three verified feature
screens → same CTA and approved destination URL.

**Test:** compare qualified demo requests by tracked entry path over a fixed evaluation window.
Record campaign and creative IDs. Exclude internal/test traffic. If tracking is incomplete,
report leads as unknown rather than attributing them to a specific ad.

**Visual identity:** supplied V79 logo, real app screenshots, recognisable Hub and Tiquet
labels/icons, accessible contrast, mobile-readable typography, uncluttered cards, visible
primary CTA. Use authorised company assets only; do not invent product screenshots.

## UI/UX direction (recommendation, not implemented by this playbook)

Simplify first-level navigation to **Overview | Campaigns | Calendar | Leads | Results | Connections**
with Brand Kit, Business Memory, Studio and settings as context/tools in the relevant section.
Show readiness and next action before advanced reporting; use one recognisable V79 light
design language shared with Hub and Tiquet. Retain keyboard navigation, focus states,
mobile support and per-channel authorisation controls.

The One Idea builder is a first small step. A future workflow should connect saved plans
to channel-ready drafts, real media checks, approval history, outcome-linked leads,
clear channel errors and verified provider results. Do not build fake dashboards or
assume a social API supports a format or capability without testing.

## REDTEAM / release gates

- **Truth:** human-verify all claims and current screenshots against approved versions.
- **Safety:** keep provider OAuth, tenant boundaries, consent and owner approval.
- **Reliability:** no automatic retry when a provider outcome is uncertain; flag reconnection.
- **Attribution:** no unverified conversions or reach; avoid double counting.
- **Usability:** novice completion test from brief to saved plan; mobile, desktop, keyboard.
- **Operations:** PR CI and isolated regression first. No live migration/deploy while
  backups or portfolio release are in a HOLD state.
